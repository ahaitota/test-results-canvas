# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

This project exists to be useful to anyone who writes tests, regardless of
experience level, background, identity or the tools they use. Everyone taking
part — issue reporters, contributors, reviewers and maintainers — is expected to
help keep it a place where people are comfortable participating.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful and assume good faith. Most disagreements come from missing
  context, not bad intent.
- Give feedback on the work, not the person. "This parser will break on nested
  suites" is useful; "you clearly didn't think about this" is not.
- Accept that maintainers may decline a change. Scope and maintenance cost are
  legitimate reasons, and a "no" is not a judgement of the contributor.
- Be patient. This is a volunteer-maintained project and responses can take
  time.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, intimidation, or personal attacks of any kind.
- Discriminatory or demeaning comments relating to age, body size, disability,
  ethnicity, gender identity or expression, level of experience, nationality,
  personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity or orientation.
- Sexualised language or imagery, or unwelcome sexual attention.
- Publishing others' private information — physical or email addresses, or
  anything scraped from an attached test report — without explicit permission.
- Sustained disruption of discussions, or deliberately derailing issues and pull
  requests.

## Scope

This applies in every project space — issues, pull requests, discussions, commit
messages and code review — and whenever someone is representing the project in
public.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour by contacting one of the maintainers privately
through their GitHub profile. Reports are handled confidentially, and you can
expect an acknowledgement within 7 days.

When reporting, please include what happened, where it happened (links to the
issue, pull request or comment if applicable), and anything else you think is
relevant. You do not need to have a formal complaint prepared — if something
felt wrong, that is enough to raise it.

Maintainers may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code, issues and pull
requests that violate this Code of Conduct, and may temporarily or permanently
ban any contributor for behaviour they consider inappropriate, threatening or
harmful. Where practical, an explanation will be given.
